# Vision of the Bizen DAO
Let's contribute to the promotion and preservation of the traditional craft culture of Bizen ware, which is one of the Six Ancient Kilns of Japan Heritage and has a history of 1000 years!

# About Bizen DAO
This is a DAO that allows potters, kilns, galleries who share the above vision to interact with fans from all over the world and enjoy Bizen ware even more. The administrators and supporters who create this system are also members of the DAO.
If you own Bizen ware with our NFT, you are a member of BizenDAO!
Anyone, whether they have NFTs or not, can join our Discord.
